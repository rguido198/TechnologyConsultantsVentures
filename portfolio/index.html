Commercial real estate landlord client · 2025–present

Autonomous operations for a commercial real estate portfolio

The problem: a commercial plaza landlord ran maintenance, leasing, and shared-expense billing entirely by hand — tickets triaged by memory, lease conflicts caught only if someone happened to notice, CAM charges reconciled manually once a year.

What I built: three named autonomous agents wired into a shared operations platform — a maintenance dispatcher that triages tickets by severity/SLA and attributes cost against the actual lease clause, a lease screener that checks every incoming request against active tenants' exclusive-use clauses in real time, and a CAM allocator that prorates shared-expense invoices by leased square footage and runs annual estimated-vs-actual reconciliation automatically. Every action that touches money or a lease commitment is gated behind human approval.

Result: processing 100+ maintenance tickets a month, running fully on the landlord's own team — no hands-on involvement from me required to keep it operating.

Agentic pipelines · Human-in-the-loop approval gates · Real-time lease-clause matching

Restaurant group client · 2024–2025

AP Control Tower — invoice-to-pay automation

The problem: invoice processing was fully manual — a bookkeeper opened every vendor invoice, validated it by hand, and keyed it into NetSuite. ~$10 in labor cost per invoice, 24-hour turnaround.

What I built: an agent pipeline that ingests CFDI 4.0 invoices, validates them against Mexican tax-authority rules, runs a 3-way match (PO / goods-receipt / invoice) against NetSuite via its RESTlet API, and routes anything outside tolerance to a human for approval — nothing posts automatically.

Result: cost per invoice dropped ~88% (~$10 → ~$1.20). Turnaround dropped from 24 hours to 3 minutes.

CFDI 4.0 validation · 3-way match · Oracle NetSuite (RESTlet/API)
